# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in communication.
- Assume good intent and provide helpful feedback.
- Focus on ideas, not personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory language.
- Doxxing, intimidation, or threats.
- Repeated disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block contributors
who violate this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately through the security reporting path in `SECURITY.md`.
